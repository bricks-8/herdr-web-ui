### Added
- The split view takes more than two tabs, side by side and stacked: drop a sidebar row or a tab
  on a slot's edge to open it beside, above or below that slot, or on its middle to open it in its
  place, up to six slots. Drag the boundary between slots, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to
  resize them. A slot moved to another place keeps its terminal, and a split kept by an earlier
  version opens as before.

### Fixed
- A pane dragged over a message box no longer leaves the box outlined as if a file were being
  dropped on it.
