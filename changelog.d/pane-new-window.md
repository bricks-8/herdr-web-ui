### Added
- A pane moves to a window of its own, to place on another screen and split there again: Move to a
  new window on a split slot's bar, or let a tab or a sidebar row go outside the window. Its slot
  here closes; a pane shown alone stays here too. On a desktop, More's Open in a new window opens
  the selected pane in one and leaves this window as it is. The new window starts with the pane
  alone and keeps its own split. A browser that blocks pop-ups is named over the pane, and nothing
  here changes.
